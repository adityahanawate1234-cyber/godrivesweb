Legal & Customer Policies

Privacy Policy

At GoDrives (operated by ADISS SELF DRIVE CAR), we respect your privacy and protect your personal data. We collect your name, phone number, email address, Driving License, and Government ID strictly for identity verification, vehicle rental compliance, and booking communication. Payment transactions are securely processed via Razorpay; we do not store your card or UPI PIN details on our servers. We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Terms & Conditions

  • The renter must be at least 21 years old and hold a valid original Indian Light Motor Vehicle (LMV) Driving License.
  • Vehicles must not be used for commercial taxi operations, racing, towing, or carrying illegal goods.
  • Fuel is provided on a level-to-level basis; any shortage at the time of return will be charged as per actuals.
  • Traffic challans, tolls, and parking fees incurred during the trip duration are the sole responsibility of the renter.
  • Late return beyond the 1-hour grace period will attract hourly extension charges as displayed in the app.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • More than 24 hours before pickup: 100% refund of the booking amount (minus nominal payment gateway fees if applicable).
  • Between 6 to 24 hours before pickup: 50% of the rental amount will be refunded.
  • Less than 6 hours before pickup / No-show: No refund on the rental booking fee; 100% of the security deposit is refunded.
  • Security Deposit & Refund Timeline: Refundable security deposits and eligible cancellation refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after vehicle inspection.
